01 The idea

Going out on your own shouldn’t mean carrying it all.

A lease. A fit-out. Business rates, utilities, insurance, the deposit, the personal guarantee. The cost of a place to call your own has kept brilliant independents at the kitchen table — or on somebody else’s terms — for far too long.

Adjoin splits the space, and the bill, between a hand-picked group of businesses who lift each other up. A returning-to-work hairdresser, a personal trainer, a software developer and a ceramicist under one beautiful roof — each with their own zone, all sharing the cost, the footfall and the good days.

It’s the freedom of your own front door, without the weight of the whole building.

Up to 8 complementary businesses share a single space
1 transparent monthly fee — rent, rates, bills, Wi-Fi, cleaning

Common partner questions

How do I get paid?

We agree a clear, straightforward arrangement up front. Typically Adjoin takes on the space, curates and manages the businesses within it, and you receive a dependable return on your building — we’ll set out the exact structure for your property before anything is signed.

What’s the commitment?

We size the agreement around your building and your plans. The aim is a partnership that genuinely works for you — we’d far rather earn a long relationship than tie you into one, and every term is laid out plainly.

Who manages the tenants and the day-to-day?

We do. Adjoin handles curation, agreements, maintenance, cleaning, community and the general running of the building — so it stays a place people want to be, without adding anything to your workload.

05 Questions

The things everyone asks.

How do the costs actually work?

You pay one transparent monthly fee for your zone, covering your share of rent, business rates, utilities, Wi-Fi, cleaning and the shared spaces. The figure scales with the size of your zone and its location — and you’ll always have the full number before you commit, with nothing hidden.

Am I tied into a long lease?

No. Adjoin runs on flexible, rolling terms designed for independent businesses. You can scale up, scale down, or move on with proper notice — never a decade-long commitment.

Who else will be in my space?

A small, curated and complementary mix. We never place directly competing businesses side by side — the whole point is that your neighbours help your business, not fight it.

What kinds of business fit?

Hair & beauty, wellbeing and therapy, fitness and movement, makers and creatives, small studios and desk-based independents. If your work benefits from a great space and passing trade, it likely fits.

What about insurance and liability?

Each business holds its own insurance for its own work, exactly as you would anywhere. Adjoin manages the building, the shared areas and cover for the common spaces. We’ll walk you through it clearly before you join.

Which locations are you in?

We’re actively developing spaces now. Register your interest and tell us where you’re based — we’ll match you to the nearest space as it comes together.
