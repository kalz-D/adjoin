How we handle your details.

What we collect

If you use the enquiry form, we receive your name, email, optional mobile number and what you tell us about your business, the space you need, or your property. It's stored in Netlify Forms and in our email.

Member profiles

If you create a member account, we store your email, a securely hashed password (we never see or keep the password itself), and the profile, photos and links you add. Your profile is only shown to other signed-in members, and only after we've reviewed it and welcomed you in. It isn't public and search engines can't see it. If you ask to be introduced to another member, we keep that request and your message so we can make the introduction. You can edit your profile at any time and delete your account, profile and photos yourself from your profile page.

Why we collect it

To reply to you, and to plan where our spaces go and who they suit. We use suburb, space and budget answers in aggregate to decide where to open. We don't sell your details or add you to a marketing list.

How long we keep it

While we're talking with you, then for up to 24 months unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold by emailing hello@adjoin.com.au. We aim to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. If you're not happy with how we've handled it, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
