Good questions.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the building. Commercial rents vary a lot by suburb and size, so there's no set price. What stays the same is the idea: you share one lease with one to four other businesses, so you carry your share rather than the whole thing. You'll see the full monthly figure before you commit.

What's included?

It varies by building, and we'll spell it out for each one. Typically internet and water are included, and power is metered and split fairly by use.

Am I locked in?

No. Every placement starts with a three-month trial. If it's working for you and the group, you stay on flexible terms.

Who will I be sharing with?

One to four businesses we've matched with you. We group people by how they work: studios and desks together, boutique services together, workshops and trades together. Within that, we look for work that complements yours and never put direct competitors side by side. You meet everyone before you move in.

What kind of buildings?

Modern single-storey industrial units, from compact ones to larger spaces: high ceilings, good light, roller-door access and parking. We fit them out so they feel like somewhere you'd want to bring a client.

Will there be shared areas?

Usually, in some form. It might be a kitchen, a meeting table, a bench everyone borrows or just a good spot for lunch. Each group decides what it needs, and it's designed in from the start.

Where are you?

We're forming our first groups in Newcastle and the Hunter. Tell us your suburb in the form and we'll use it to decide where our first buildings go.

What about insurance?

You cover your own business, as you would anywhere. Adjoin looks after the building and the shared areas. We'll take you through it before you sign anything.
