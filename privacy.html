Privacy

How we use what you send us.

Adjoin is a project of Care in Movement. Enquiries go to connect@careinmovement.com.

What we collect

If you use the form, we receive your name, email, optional phone number, and whatever you tell us about your business or property. That sits in Netlify Forms and in our email. We do not sell it, and we do not add you to a marketing list.

Why

To reply to your enquiry and, if you ask us to, match you with a space or a partnership conversation. That is a legitimate interest in responding to a request you made.

How long

We keep enquiry details while we are in conversation, then for up to 24 months unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete what we hold. Email the address above. If you are in the UK you can also complain to the ICO.
