# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences, one site:

- Independent businesses (hairdressers, barbers, trainers, makers, therapists, studio and desk workers) who need a real commercial home without a decade-long lease, personal guarantee, or going it alone.
- Space partners and landlords sitting on empty or underused commercial units who want occupancy, yield, and a managed mix without running it themselves.

They arrive on a phone or laptop, often from a search or a share, and decide in one sitting whether to enquire.

## Product Purpose

Adjoin is a marketing site that sells the model: curated, beautifully designed commercial spaces shared by complementary independents. Success is a qualified enquiry (business or partner) via the Netlify form or email. The space planner exists to make the cost-sharing idea felt, not to quote a real rent.

## Brand Personality

Architectural, neighbourly, exact. Voice is plain British English, no coworking slang, no hype. The feeling should be: a well-made high-street building with the door open, not a WeWork party or a landlord brochure.

## Anti-references

- WeWork-era beer taps, neon, and “community-adjusted EBITDA”
- Generic SaaS coworking landings (icon grids, purple gradients, Inter-on-white dashboards)
- Estate-agent listing pages
- Food-hall / Boxpark event energy as the whole brand

## Design Principles

- Show the mix, don’t just describe it — people, trades, and a floor, not desks as a lifestyle.
- One monthly number, one door: every screen should make sharing feel cheaper and less lonely than going alone.
- Architectural restraint over decoration: hairline rules, tight type, clay used sparingly.
- Two doors, one form: businesses and landlords share the page without mixing their jobs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA. Body and form placeholder text must stay above 4.5:1. Honour `prefers-reduced-motion` (no marquee, ken-burns, or scroll-reveal motion). Keyboard and screen-reader paths through the menu, planner, FAQs, and form are first-class. Language is `en-GB`.
