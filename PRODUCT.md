## What Adjoin is

Adjoin takes the lease on modern single-storey industrial units in Newcastle and the Hunter, fits them out, and shares each one between two to five complementary businesses. Adjoin earns the margin between the head lease and what members pay, and adds value through design, fit-out and matching the right people.

Stage: pre-launch demand testing. No live sites yet. The site must never imply there are.

## Users (in priority order)

1. Freelancers and micro businesses who need a real space but not a whole unit.
2. Makers, trainers and studios who need floor space, clearance and noise tolerance.
3. Side hustles ready to move out of the garage.
4. Companies wanting desks for Hunter-based remote staff.
5. Landlords of industrial units (dedicated section, secondary audience).

## Core message

Industrial units are sized for fleets, not freelancers. Share one. The market problems are too big, too pricey, too long and too quiet; Adjoin fixes all four.

Process: tell us what you need, co-design your section, meet your people, move in. Every placement starts with a three-month trial.

Pricing is shown only as a share of the lease (1/2 to 1/5), never in dollars, because it depends on the building.

Founding members: locked-in rate and a co-designed section.

## Voice

Australian English. Polished and architectural in structure, warm and optimistic in content. Plain verbs, sentence case, no slang, no hype, no coworking clichés.

## Visual system

Hard vs soft. Industrial elements (plans, steps, rules) are square-edged; human elements (buttons, cards, the lounge, photos) are rounded.
Energy: charcoal #232427 + signal orange #FF5F1F. Calm: eucalypt #2E4A3E / #6F8F7D, sandstone #E6CFA8 / #F2E7D4, off-white #F5F4F0.
Type: Bricolage Grotesque (display, uses its width axis for rhythm) + Instrument Sans (body).
Signature: the lease splitter in the hero. Keep other motion minimal.
Imagery: architectural line drawings plus atmospheric photos, always labelled as illustrative until real sites exist.

## Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA. Honour prefers-reduced-motion. Keyboard paths through the menu, splitter (native radios), FAQ and form are first-class. Language is en-AU.
